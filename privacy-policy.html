Privacy Policy

Amanah Operations Department

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Amanah Operations Department ("Amanah," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our website, online store, administrative tools, customer messaging features, or Facebook-connected marketing features.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account and administrator information: name, email address, account identifier, and authentication-related information.
  • Order information: customer name, mobile or confirmation phone number, delivery address, selected products or bundles, quantity, payment method, delivery notes, order status, and order timestamps.
  • Customer support messages: name provided to start a conversation, message contents, conversation identifiers, and message timestamps.
  • Facebook and Page information: Facebook account or user identifiers, authorized Page names and identifiers, permissions, and access credentials needed to provide the Facebook features you authorize.
  • Content you submit: post text, photographs, and other media you choose to upload or publish.
  • Technical information: information needed to operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the services.

2. How We Use Information

We use information as needed to operate the services, including to:

  • authenticate users and protect administrator-only functions;
  • receive and process orders and coordinate delivery or payment;
  • provide customer support and maintain conversation history;
  • connect Facebook Pages that an authorized user selects;
  • create and publish posts or photographs when an authorized user requests those actions;
  • maintain security, investigate errors, and improve service reliability; and
  • respond to privacy requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Facebook and Meta Platform Data

Our Facebook-connected features may use Facebook Login and the Meta Graph API. If you connect a Facebook account or Page, we access only the information and permissions made available through the authorization you grant and use that access to provide the requested Page-management and publishing features.

You can revoke the application's access through your Facebook settings. You may also contact us to request that we disconnect your Page or delete associated information. Use of Facebook is also governed by Meta's own terms and policies.

4. How Information Is Shared

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website and its features, including Supabase for authentication, database, and application infrastructure. Information may also be shared when you direct us to publish content to Facebook, when necessary to provide a requested service, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, or our services.

Amanah does not sell personal information.

5. Storage and Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. Access to information is limited to authorized users and application processes where appropriate. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, protect the services, and meet legal requirements. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable obligations.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information associated with your use of Amanah. You may also disconnect a Facebook account or Page or revoke its permissions through Facebook settings. Some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

8. Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information, email amanahoperationsdept@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Include the account email or relevant Facebook Page name so we can identify the information. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secret credentials. We will review the request and respond, subject to identity verification and applicable legal retention requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Our administrative and Facebook marketing features are intended for authorized adult business or organizational users. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information through these features.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
